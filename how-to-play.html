Firefighter Spelling — How to play

Same game on phone, tablet, classroom PC, or laptop. Open the game.

  1. Tap TAP TO PLAY so Grandma can talk (voices are baked mp3 files, not internet TTS).
  2. Watch the windows. Tap the next letter of the word.
  3. Right letter = the ladder slams and you climb.
  4. Wrong letter = stay and try that floor again.
  5. Beat the fire bar. Reach Grandma.
  6. If it burns, watch the big letters, then try again or skip.
  7. Rescue every word. Play again from the results screen.

Phone / tablet: tap a window.

Computer: keys 1–5 pick windows. Enter starts / try again / play again.

The word list comes from packs/numbers-en.json (one, two, three, four, five). You can paste your own list, but new words only speak if a matching baked mp3 exists.
